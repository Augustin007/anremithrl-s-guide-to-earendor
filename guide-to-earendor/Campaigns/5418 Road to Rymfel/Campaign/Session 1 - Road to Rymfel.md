| ——— |[[Road to Rymfel]]|[[Session 2 - Road to Rymfel]]|

De'enor Sable is an intern working for the Tal'berian Labratory under the mentorship Nehe'ara D'yth. He is alone in the laboratory, doing the closing shift. There's a beaker stuck in another beaker as he's taking them out of the steriliser, the heat having slightly fused their bottoms together. Trying to separate them, he trips and smashes them.

He opens the broom closet to see a kid wearing a black coat and lockpicking tools in his hands. The kid immediately steps out, distracting him from missing the other one. also hiding in the closet.

The kid tries to steal the keys multiple times, and claims he's a forty-year-old janitor, hired by Nehe'ara D'yth. 

De'enor calls his mentor via sending stone to ask about the kid. The kid makes chaos and lies through the stone at Nehe'ara. Nehe'ara says she's on her way. 

De'enor is quiet while the kid yaps at him. He calls himelf Sul ruc'u'ure, or "Sulruc" for short. He eventually bothers De'enor so much he shoves the kid, and the kid makes a great show of being badly hurt. Blantantly lies constantly, pockets random things and knocks equipment off a table.  De'enor notes that Nehe'ara ought to be here by now, and wants to go home to his cat.

The windows slowly go dark and another kid, a nearly identical girl, stumbles out of the closet giving warning that something is approaching. Sulruc calls her "sulrii".

A strong wind batters the door and a deep voice asks for permission to come in. De'enor thoughtlessly accepts while Sulruc and Sulrii clamor to say no, but it's too late. A dark mist seeps in and a pale man materialises.
