|[[Session 1 - Road to Rymfel]]|[[Road to Rymfel]]|[[Session 3 - Road to Rymfel]]|
Sulrii and Sulruc try to annoy what is clearly a vampire as he Silences them magically and intimidates De'enor into helping him get into the deeper layers of the building, eventually reaching the unfinished Astral Leywrite. The man also claims to have killed Nehe'ara.

Sulruc distracts Kas while Sulrii steals his sword, but he simply heats it up and they're forced to drop it. He cuts through the security, steals the Leywrite, and leaves.

As alarms are blaring, Tal'berius Tarrilion, owner of the laboratory shows up. Realising what has occurred he interrogates De'enor, Sulruc, and Sulrii. He sends them on a quest (funded by him) to secretly prepare a new laboratory for him at a different location, choosing them because they would likely not attract attention or be connected to him, while he recovers the Leywrite.

He asks for Rymfel to avoid "governmental restrictions" and promises to buy them each minor lordships in Rymfel in return.
