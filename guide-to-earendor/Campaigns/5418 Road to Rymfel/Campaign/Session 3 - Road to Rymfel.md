De'enor buys some supplies and lets Sulruc and Sulrii stay at his place. Sul'ruc eats all of De'enor's pantry, which is only a few slices of stale bread, and feeds a large quantity of treats to De'enor's cat. 

De'enor pays his neighbour the next day to take care of his cat in the meantime. He buys two horses, which Sulrii names Cutie Pie and Sweetie Pie.

They begin travelling and are ambushed by four men. De'enor flees and is shot down with arrows while Sulruc and Sulrii fight. Sulrii kills one of them after a brief skirmish while Sulruc mangles one of the attacker's arms and knocks him out. Sulruc is able to gravely injure another and he begins to flee along with his ally, but Sulrii kills them both with arrows to the back. Sulruc ties up his man and they loot the four bandits with De'enor's help. Sulrii heals De'enor. De'enor is now more convinced of his need of them, which he hadn't been before, intending to go alone.

They reach Goodburgh, what looks to be a small town with large plantations. De'enor reserves a room at the inn and stables the horses, while Sulruc and Sulrii try the local blacksmith for new weapons, who claims to not have a weapon's license. Sulrii bribes him into making her a sword by end of the day.

The three eat at the inn, Sulruc purchasing a large bottle of whiskey for them. They see an old couple in the corner and head over there. Sulruc sweet-talks the couple and offers them some to drink, wanting to hear how they met. 

The woman is eventually prevailed upon and begins to tell the story that he was a slave working on the field next to her father's plantation and he would hop over the fence to steal apples, she caught him once and thought he was handsome, so she told her father on him and he was caught and beaten, and she purchased him from the neighbours, kept him in his room and nursed him back to health, forcing him multiple times and not letting him leave until he agreed to marry her. When the father found out she was going to marry a slave, he was going to have him killed, and the two fled. They now own several of the largest plantations and 'more slaves than most'. Sulruc, whose mother was a slave, is appalled, but keeps his cool. Next to him, Sul'rii has thrown up all over herself and passed out and De'enor is very woozy, and he uses this as an excuse to have to leave.

As Sul'rii recovers, De'enor comes up with the idea to cut her hair as a prank, and Sul'ruc intervenes. As he comes down to buy an extra room so that they can be separated, De'enor trips and falls down the stairs, injuring himself, and Sul'ruc tends to his wounds.

Sul'rii eventually wakes up with a terrible hangover. She makes her way to the blacksmith and gets her sword, paying the second half. De'enor goes to eat a bit as he's recovering. Sul'ruc heads out to the plantation and meets a slave who calls himself 12124, befriending him. Sul'ruc comes up with a plan to free him and talks to the overseer.

His idea is that overseers often have the tools necessary to break the tracking spells and other slavery spells, because they can (with approval) sell a slave, but many can be prevailed to sell a slave at a markup and pretend later to the owner that the slave got injured or died. If Sul'ruc purchases the slave, then steals the coin back, the overseer can't call anyone without admitting what he did without authorisation. He plans the heist with Sulrii and borrows the coin from De'enor. They purchase 12124 and Sul'ruc immediately pickpockets the coin back, and they flee back to the inn. They return the coin to De'enor and take off on the two horses, which are tired and overburdened, The overseer, along with two accomplices, ride out after them on fresh horses, but Sul'rii snipes the overseer from six hundred feet away with an arrowshot, and the other two double back and flee. They realise now that even if no one would know to chase them over the missing slave, (which they well might), they would definitely know to chase them for the death of the overseer. 

12124 chooses the name Fere'ind "shackle-less" and they right a few hours to the main city of Elelvin, with it's tall towers and airship docks.
