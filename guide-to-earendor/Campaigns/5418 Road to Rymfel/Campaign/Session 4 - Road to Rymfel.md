They arrive at the city of Elelvin, and give their names at the front gate to the guards. Fere'ind's clothes and neck tattoo mark him as a slave, and so Sulruc gives his number, and apologises to Fere'ind once they enter the city. As they're heading in, they meet Fennic Orr, who is advertising the travelling circus. De'enor asks for lessons and Fennic  sells them on the circus and flirts with them. Sul'ruc impresses Ferrin with his wit and charm, and he agrees to meet them a few hours after the show. They agree to also go *to* the show. 

They stables the horses, Sweetie-pie and Cutie-pie, and Sulruc gives Fere'ind half of his money along with his slave-stamp, the actual object that "counts" as his ownership, which can be used to track him, and tells Fere'ind to go wild and be free. The four split up, but agree to meet at the circus.

Sulrii and De'enor end up meeting up again at a Bowyer's shop, where Sulrii has De'enor fund her purchasing an expensive 160-pound enchanted masterwork warbow for her, and De'enor picks up a light hunting bow for himself. He forgets to buy arrows and leaves while Sulrii goes to the range to try out her new bow. 

Sulruc goes to a deep-bar (for more nocturnal species, since it's early in the morning and most diurnal people are at work) where a cockfight is going to happen and places the entirety of his remaining money on 3-to-1 odds against a chicken named 'maedwyn', who belongs to a large Orc who seems to be an off-duty guard, in favour of a chicken named Foreman, belonging to a well-dressed Drow. As it's going on, he sees a human man, standing out in the bar, asking questions to some people and the people pointing to him. He tries to hide and disappear, but the man finds him. They have a brief conversation before Sulruc slips away, accidentally giving the man his name. Since he slips away, he left his money behind. 

De'enor goes to exchange some money to more useful denominations at the bank. Since he does not have an account with them he takes a 1% transaction fee. He buys some pastries at a shop when he runs into Fere'ind, who has bought new clothes to hide himself and been swindled out of some of his money whilst purchasing some whittling tools. He is buzzing with excitement at his freedom. He tries some of the pastries and immediately wants to buy some, Sulruc having given him far more money than he'd ever seen in his life.  He also points out that De'enor forgot to buy arrows and De'enor hurries off to get some.

Sulruc finds his sister and conveys to her that people are after them, and the two go and look for the others. Some time later they find Fere'ind, having spent all the money, with a bottle of strong "Bugbear Brew", meant to get Bugbears drunk, which Fere'ind barely drunk from because it actually tasted weird, a bunch of clothes and pastries and little wooden carvings all around. Sulruc takes the bottle for himself and takes a few swigs and remains surprisingly stable, he teases his sister but she refuses to drink any. Sulruc and Sulrii explain the situation to him and he sobers up, apologising for getting metaphorically drunk on his newfound freedom. 

Heading back to the circus for the show, as it's getting to that time, De'enor notices someone following him, a foreign-looking man, and he asks what it is about. The man guides him to a carriage, driven by a similarly foreign woman holding a strange metalic tube (the newfound invention: a "distancer", or gun).  Sulruc and Sulrii see he is getting tricked and captured and get the drop on them. Sulruc holding the man at daggerpoint silently in the middle of the crowded street, he convinces them to let De'enor go and asks what they're up to, and they give some information on the bounty. The bounty hunters leave, and the four of them regroup to watch the show.
