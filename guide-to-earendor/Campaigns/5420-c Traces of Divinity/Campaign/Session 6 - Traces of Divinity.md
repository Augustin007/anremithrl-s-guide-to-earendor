Gethin obtains Niamh's letter to him:

> Gethin, ever constant protector, who raised me long ago, would that I had taken your advice.
> 
> I will, no doubt, die soon, and before reaching you, but not for another few minutes, at least, Alantothi has bought me that much, and the cobalt walls were a good precaution.
> 
> I intercepted a trade between the Grey Lady and the queen. You know where to find it.
> 
> Keep on eye on Lancel. I wish I had been closer to him.
> 
> Goodbye.

Gethin leaves to get whatever-it-is that Niamh intercepted.

Norvias approaches via the air and finds the top floor of the main tower missing, having been suspended into the air as an observatory by Lancel trying to catch the killer by putting a bounty on himself. In the ensuing skirmish, Mials and Norvias destroy the floating tower and flee into the main castle. Norvias runs around the castle and its passages while Lancel reactivates the TAXBLADE. 

Norvias meets Azmodeus, who hacks the spike to cut off Norvias's communication, inserting his own words, and pretends to be helping the Den, and Norvias agrees to use the spike to summon Asmodeus when they've found Gethin, which Asmodeus declares a secondary mission by pretending to be the Koluss. 

Gething obtains the map to the Oathbreaker Sword, and takes it back, meeting up with Lancel. Lancel puts on his power armour and makes a copy of the map, while Mials and Norvias find the lair. Norvias summons Asmodeus via the spike, who hacks the TAXBLADE and captures Gethin, who destroys the map rather than give it up. Asmodeus leaves the building with Gethin and sets the TAXBLADE on Norvias, Mials, and Lancel. Mials is killed by the TAXBLADE before Lancel is able to deactivate it. Norvias and Lancel get away together and Lancel arrests Norvias but Norvias convinces Lancel that he can help him rescue Gethin.

This coincides with New Year's, so the main city is alight with celebration. It also coincides with the sudden earthquake brought on by Valthor Remshi's demise halfway across the world. Taking advantage of this chaos, attacking the political rival that Tahlia percieves in Rymfel and picking up her grandfather, Tahlia's airships arrive and fire on the city. The city defenses force them away, but severe damage is done, particularly to the docks and Moonwatcher Manor, and Asmodues has gotten away with the captive Gethin on his grandaughter's airship.
