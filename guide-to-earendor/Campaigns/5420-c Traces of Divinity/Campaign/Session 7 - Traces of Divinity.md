Lancel and Norvias make their way out of the caves. Lancel removes most of the restraints leaving them only around the neck. Lancel then asks Norvias to swear an unbreakable oath, "I will not reveal your identity as the Raven Head'. After Lancel removes his raven head armor he dirties his underclothes and takes Norvias' cloak. 

The group go to the city where there are currently new year festivities. In the town they buy themselves 2 new years tree like costumes for a total of 2 silver. 

They are then led to a sidestreet by what seem to be members of the Kollus. On the way Lancel spots the school with grean smoke coming out of it. When they reach the allyway a ton of people talk in unison telling him he is about to be questioned. A man apears from the mist and grabs Norvias, he is not physically here, but is a powerful enough manifestation to grab Norvias.

He waves for everyone to leave, leaving Norvias, Lancel, and himself alone. After a brief examination of the evidence he decides that Norvias is innocent and that the Koluss had informed Asmodeus of the mission and let him sabotage the spike, and states he will kill the Koluss for this betrayal. 

The man asks for Lancel's copy of the Oathbreaker Sword map, which he curiously notes as being his sword, leading Lancel to believe this is Kas, leader of the Den along with his wife Ashdilkir and creator of the Oathbreaker Sword. Lancel attempts to strike a bargain with him in attempt to rescue Gethin. Eventually he agrees to give the man the ichor in exchange for help saving Gethin as well as a primordial dragon scale and being allowed to keep his map of the oathbreaker sword. The men shake hands and agree to exchange gifts in person, near where Kas assumes Gethin is being taken, the Kharrish cave system. The illusion dissapears, leaving them alone in the alleyway.
