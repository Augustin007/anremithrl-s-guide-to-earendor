They head to the docks. Lancel makes a show of helping the rescue efforts. He talks to the local military commander about the attack and has military ships head in to help transport men for the rescue effort. 

He reserves the Iron Barrage, the most heavily armed battleship of his armada for the journey to Kharr. Taking advantage of the shock post Tahlia's attack, and using Tahlia's attack as an excuse, he declares war on Tahlia. He sends a force to capture Blackton and his uncle, and uses shock doctrine to order his soldiers to arrest his political rivals, including Almak.
