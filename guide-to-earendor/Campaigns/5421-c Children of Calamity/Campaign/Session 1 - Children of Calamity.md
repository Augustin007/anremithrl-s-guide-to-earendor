|———|[[Children of Calamity]]|[[Session 2 - Children of Calamity]]|

Reivhen visits her father in prison. He is mean to her, triggering the ire of the inmate Saelinor Teson, who was receiving as a visitor his own friend Erikson, and the two confront Reivhen's father. Before a scuffle can erupt, the Shattering occurs, creating a blinding light as the sky erupts in a quiddic-annihilation reaction. In the moments of confusion, Domun Yen attacks the prison, killing Erikson and many of the guards. Saelinor brings Reivhen along to keep her safe as he escapes the prison. The two befriend each other quickly. The prison and boats all vanish, leaving them on the penninsula alone, where they rest for some time before a scrying eye locates them. They flee to the nearest city, where they meet a baker named Hilda. They ask her for directions and are able to locate the local branch of the Honourable Party, the political party led by Saelinor Teson before his imprisonment, now led by Dylan Farnson. At the local branch, Saelinor sees Cormac "the Older" Arnborn, who informs him that they were planning to break him out, and hence the 'main crew' is at the city. They eat Beef Borginon and drink along with the party before they go to bed, planning now to flee south for a bit to meet with Oisin Flinboth, who is finishing his doctorate. They catch up with Dylan and Cormac "the younger" Lagr, planning their next moves and having a small funeral for Erikson. Arnborn gives Reivhen a book on knifework. Everyone returns home to sleep while Reivhen and Saelinor sleep in Arnborn's guest rooms. Arnborn demonstrates a lot of forgetfulness in his older age and Saelinor helps him a bit.
