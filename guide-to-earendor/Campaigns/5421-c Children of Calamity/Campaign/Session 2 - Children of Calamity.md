Arnborn wakes the two of them up, insulting Saelinor teasingly and making them breakfast and packing them food for their trip. He is the first to notice the Incineration, the sky burning up and cracking to thin borders between the planes, leaving a golden ash-like residue. He forces Reivhen to eat properly.  He gives her his old daggers and returns to Saelinor his hammer.

Lagr arrives with three horses and they ride out to the river. Reivhen doesn't know how to ride on her own and Lagr leads her and Plod. The river is choking up with ash and the boat is nowhere to be seen, and they discuss it and decide to travel some more. 

The ash continues piling up.

They cross the border into Drakenthorne and are accosted by scout of the Kharazian forces. As Saelinor and Cormac talk them down, Reivhen kills the captain, causing a battle. Saelinor enters a beserk rage in this time, and as the scouts retreat to get reinforcements, Saelinor attacks Cormac.
