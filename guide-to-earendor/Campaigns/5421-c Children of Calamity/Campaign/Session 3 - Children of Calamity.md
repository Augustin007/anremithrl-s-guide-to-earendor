Saelinor kills Cormac while under the curse, and Rhaevhen stops him by stabbing his knee. He passes out.

Kharazien soldiers arrive and capture them all. These are soldiers under House Durnhal, and they are taken to the Seat of Durnmarch, where Saelinor's knee is amputated and they are locked in prison and questioned. Saelinor notes the lack of magic.
