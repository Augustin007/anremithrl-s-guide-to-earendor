Raevhen was a Karrish girl whose parents abused her. She would visit her father in Verenbright jail on the moment of the Shattering, and Saelinor stepped in to defend her from her father and again during the ensuing attack by Domun Yen. She would bond to Saelinor and the Honourable Party, and begin travelling to Neros along with Cormac and Saelinor. She would attack a group of training Kharazien scouts and get the party attacked and arrested, and Cormac died here.
