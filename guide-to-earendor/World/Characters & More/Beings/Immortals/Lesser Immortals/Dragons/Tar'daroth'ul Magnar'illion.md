Magnar was a Dravaran elf and sorcerer who was gifted dragonhood by Tiamat and Bahamut. He was the first lesser being turned into a dragon. He would at some point become the figurehead of a rebellion against the tyrant Tar-Nakthos, and after this be chosen king.

He is the father of [[Tal'berius Tarrilion]] and [[Artorius]].

He would die in the sundering war fighting alongside his children against Tharizdun.
