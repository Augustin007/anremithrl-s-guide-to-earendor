Nak of None was a lowborn orphan in southern Karr in 4608 AS. He had been joined to the army of House Remshi, and was in the castle when it was sacked in 4622. He took advantage of the chaos during the invasion to kill off the youngest Remshi. When the invaders couldn't find the last Remshi, they reported he got away, and Nak took on the identity of the Valthor Remshi. Throughout the wars, Remshi slowly climbed the ranks. He used chaos and confusion to his advantage. He learned to lie, cheat, and steal to gain more money and power for himself. He married Lady Sai'ren'illi purely for political power, and had a number of mistresses. Sai'ren'illi was barren, and bore him no heirs, and upon discovering this, he murdered her. In 4635 he poisoned King Tar'len'ram of Karr the day before the king's own wedding, hoping to take more advantage of the ensuing war. In that time, he also shortly seduced and was betrothed the king's ex-fiancée Vra'nel'is, though she committed suicide before he was able to marry her.

 He caught the attention of Bechel at around this time.

In the summer of 4639, taking advantage of the existing succession war, [[Tahlia Tormund]] and [[Bechel of Bharam]] began to plan an invasion of Karr. Bechel noticed him and was impressed, and therefore warned him of their impending invasion He quickly realised they were going to win and sided with them, as Bechel had hoped.

He and Bechel became friends and he encouraged her betrayal of Asmodeus. She knew that he encouraged it for his own gain. In the subsequent power vacuum as Tahlia chased Bechel into the nine hells, he began carving out an area in the northwestern regions of Karr for himself, Tiamat began to speak to him, and he intimidated Vorrak until she granted true dragonhood. He created a brood of lesser dragons out of his loyal followers and they carved out the kingdom of Drakenthorne, and he crowned himself king in the year 4645 AS.

He married [[Maraveth Kalagiano]] during this campaign to gain the political alliance of Solenthir, and she bore him five children: [[Kae'dros'vro'tha'ne Remshi'ul]], [[Madhra'vel'thyn Remshi'il]], [[Ot'vith'drin'tar Remshi'ul]], [[Sa'reth'vro'drel Remshi'il]], [[Vel'arra'ka'veth'tar Remshi'il]]. 

After Maraveth died, he was seduced by [[Iti'esse Kyr'ash]], and she was shortly with child. Her position as a direct daughter of one of his more loyal followers made it seem to him a bad move politically to allow chaos, he was already at the top, now chaos could only lead to downfall, and so he married her to avoid scandal. She mothered [[Ma'vro'ric'kath Remshi'il]], and [[Ka'vral'tar Remshi'il]], but was poisoned by political rivals. During this time, Valthor also fathered a bastard daughter, who Iti'esse raised [[Mael'thene'vel'dral Dan'il]].

The dragons, combined with the generally large amount of draconic magic in the region, turned the population into one of dragonborn over the course of a few centuries. Valthor spent the those centuries consolidating his power and killed off the lesser dragons he had created, and began establishing stability. 

House Kar'va poisoned Queen Iti'esse, and eventually offered in recompense to Remshi [[Rha'enna Kar'va]], who did not wish to marry him. She bore him four sons: [[Ser'alyn'vel'kara Remshi'ul]], [[Tae'vric'drin'vor'tar Remshi'ul]], [[Jo'rath'ka'rol Remshi'ul]], and [[Dar Remshi'ul]], who died as an infant. Between Taevric and Jorath, was born [[Vaedric'ka'dros Dan'il]] to a Valthor and a slave. Rha'enna hated him and killed herself after Dar's death. 

He fathered [[Dar'r'an'nil'vran Dan'ul]] to a prostitute in the subsequent time.

He would go on to marry [[Ceira Valmyr]] in 4900, and she bore him [[Rae'lith'vel'shae Remshi'ul]]. He also fathered the bastard [[Lethara of None]]. Ceira was a spy for another country, and upon discovering this, he had her executed.

[[Mae'rys Dro'vann]] married him, took money, and then ran away. This would cause a small war, but Valthor was dealing with too much internal political strife at the time, and consolidating his new shipping companies. In about this same time, Valthor tried to open of the Trees of Sanctuary to let Tiamat through, having a plan to capture her. This went badly and Auril went through and escaped him. She would begin her invasion shortly thereafter. 

He married [[Valdynne Saeryn]], who bore him first three sons [[Cal'bet'drin'vael Remshi'ul]], [[Dor'nic'ka'jor Remshi'il]],  [[Thel'rya'vel'thir Remshi'ul]] before the birth of the bastard [[Calvin Redwood]], who she also raised, and then three after that: [[Vaer'eth'vro'kae Remshi'ul]], [[Ve'dros Remshi'il]], and [[Lo'ric'ka'drath Remshi'ul]]. He became paranoid that she was cheating on him and that the sons were not his own, and he killed her, his sons, and Calvin.

He had many more bastards he did not claim. 

Some years later he married [[Emma Miraleth]] in order to gain her family's support against the dwarves, aiding her family in conquering the Deathwood from the bugbears clans, she was well-beloved. To her was born [[Nil'ge'tar Remshi'ul]]. Remshi eventually turned on her family and snatched this land for himself, and she killed herself. He reported it as a Durinic assasination, stoking hatred and support for the dwarven-dragonborn war. 

He took [[Cae'ryn Tol]], a merchant's daughter and from her came [[Tar'id'lion'il Remshi'ul]]. 

Teetering on the edge of losing power and the war, and losing support from Xirdakul, he reached a truce with the dwarves, and was forced to exile his son Nil'ge'tar who continuously broke the truce. Doing this, he married Car'ryn and legitimised Tar'id'lion'il. 

He dispelled his son Nil'ge'tar's ghost before it could reveal that he released Auril. 

He created new dragons during the War of Names, and used them to defend the Trees of Sanctuary, protecting the world for a bit longer, until he was defeated and killed, causing [[The Earthquake]]. He delayed [[The Shattering]] by several months.
