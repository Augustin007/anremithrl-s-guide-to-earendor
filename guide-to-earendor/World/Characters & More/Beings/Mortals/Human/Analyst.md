The Analyst was initially an intern hired by [[Baron Daron]] in 5419 AS to handle some of his work as CFO of [[DragonArrow]]. Since Baron Daron was not allowed to hire an intern, Baron rehired him as his personal assistant and payed him himself, but still called him "The Intern". 

The Intern would eventually refuse to give his name, only known as Intern, and became fiercly loyal to Baron Daron. The two were able to together swindle the Hidden Thieves out of Asmodean Ichor. 

When Baron Daron brought him to an important strategy meeting, he quickly reformatted Intern's role to "Analyst", and the name stuck. Analyst would become a key part of several strategy meetings.

Analyst was hurt by Baron Daron's death and drunk himself into a stupor, nearly dying of alchohol poisoning. The next day, during the Battle of the Shattering, the drunk Analyst would be confronted by Kas and Ashdilkir. He would bravely stand up to them and their words would enable him to make the connection about how the labyrinth stone works, and he would rush to convey it to William Treaty. 

He would die in the Shattering.
